$help • Sends all commands to your private chat. 
$wa / $h / $m • Draws a Waifu, Husbando, or Both. 
$mmi / $mml • Opens your harem (Images / Text). 
$im / $ima • Searches for Character / Series information. 
$top • Shows the most wanted characters globally. 
$wish / $wishlist • Wishes for a character / Shows your wishlist. 
$like / $likelist • Likes a character / Shows your favorites. 
$note [name]$[text] • Adds a custom note. 
$divorce [name] • Divorces a character. 
$rnl / $rnw • Renames your Harem or Wishlist. 
$sm$mmk= / $sm note • Sorts by Kakera / Pushes notes to the top. 
$k/$kakera / $dailykakera $dk $daily • Rank up and check status / collect daily kakera
